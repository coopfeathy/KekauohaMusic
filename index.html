About

Conor Kekauoha's Private Music Studio

Conor Kekauoha's Private Music Studio is a dedicated teaching studio offering personalized instruction in guitar, ukulele, piano, voice, and music theory. Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or refining advanced technique, every lesson is crafted to meet you exactly where you are.

With a philosophy grounded in patience, creativity, and cultural connection, the studio fosters an environment where curiosity thrives and every student feels supported on their musical journey. Located in Manhattan Beach, we serve the surrounding South Bay communities and offer virtual lessons worldwide.

Studio Info

Studio Policies & Rates

Conor Kekauoha's Private Music Studio — Serving Manhattan Beach & Surrounding Areas

Effective January 1, 2026

Lesson Formats & Travel Policy

Lessons are offered:

  • In-home (within approved travel radius)
  • Virtual (Zoom/FaceTime)

Guardian Presence Policy:

  • For students under 16, a parent/guardian/nanny must be present for the full duration of all lessons (in-home & virtual).
  • For students ages 16–17, parent/guardian presence is required unless otherwise arranged on a case-by-case basis.

Travel Boundaries:

The teacher maintains a maximum travel radius of 20 minutes from Manhattan Beach to keep scheduling consistent and sustainable.

Families outside this range may be offered:

  • Virtual lessons, or
  • In-home lessons at an adjusted travel rate (if approved)
  • Rates will be quoted on a case-by-case basis based on distance and scheduling availability

Lesson Lengths, Rates, & Monthly Tuition

Standard Rates (In-Home / Virtual):

  • 60 minutes: $100
  • 45 minutes: $80
  • 30 minutes: $60

45-minute lessons are preferred for in-home instruction. 30-minute in-home lessons may be offered at the teacher's discretion based on scheduling and location.

Monthly invoices reflect the number of scheduled lessons in that month. Because some months include five lesson weeks, monthly invoices may be higher in 5-week months.

Rate Structure

Rates reflect instruction time, lesson planning, individualized coaching, and reserving a consistent weekly lesson slot. Rates apply equally to in-home and virtual lessons because the quality of instruction, preparation, and teacher time remain the same regardless of location.

Payment Policy

  • Tuition is due on the first lesson of each month.
  • Payments more than 5 days late may incur a $15 late fee.
  • Tuition is non-refundable, since lesson time is reserved weekly.

Accepted payment methods: Zelle, Venmo (@Conor-Kekauoha), cash, or check

Cancellations & Make-Up Lessons

When a student registers, a weekly lesson time is reserved. Missed lessons impact both teacher scheduling and student progress.

24-Hour Cancellation Policy:

  • 24+ hours notice: eligible for make-up
  • Less than 24 hours notice: lesson is forfeited

No Shows: Missed lessons due to no-shows are forfeited and cannot be made up.

Make-Up Lessons: Each student is eligible for one (1) make-up lesson per month with 24+ hours notice.

Make-ups must follow these guidelines:

  • Must be completed within 30 days
  • Make-up lessons are not guaranteed and are offered as availability allows
  • May be completed virtually if needed

Illness: If a student is sick day-of, the lesson may be converted to a virtual lesson at the scheduled lesson time. If virtual is declined, the cancellation follows the standard 24-hour policy.

Teacher Cancellations: If the teacher must cancel due to illness or emergency, students will receive:

  • A make-up lesson, or
  • A credit applied to the next month's tuition

Ending Lessons / Withdrawal Policy

Because weekly lesson times are reserved, 30 days notice is required to discontinue lessons. Ending without notice may result in responsibility for tuition through the end of the month and/or an additional month of tuition.
