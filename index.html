About

Kekauoha Music

Kekauoha Music is a dedicated teaching studio offering personalized instruction in guitar, ukulele, piano, voice, and music theory. Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or refining advanced technique, every lesson is crafted to meet you exactly where you are.

With a philosophy grounded in patience, creativity, and cultural connection, Kekauoha Music fosters an environment where curiosity thrives and every student feels supported on their musical journey.

🎸

Guitar & Ukulele

Acoustic, electric, classical, and Hawaiian slack-key styles for all levels.

🎹

Piano & Keys

Classical foundations, contemporary pop, and jazz harmony.

🎤

Voice

Technique, breath control, performance confidence, and repertoire development.

🎵

Music Theory

Ear training, notation, harmony, and composition fundamentals.

Ground Rules

Studio Policies

These policies keep the studio a respectful, productive space for everyone. Please read before booking.

📅

Scheduling & Booking

  • Lessons must be booked at least 24 hours in advance through the booking form or by direct contact.
  • Recurring weekly slots are available and recommended for consistent progress.
  • Availability is shown on the booking form; first-come, first-served.
❌

Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations made 48+ hours before a session are fully refunded or rescheduled at no charge.
  • Cancellations within 24–48 hours may be rescheduled once at no charge; late cancellations forfeit the session fee.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are non-refundable.
  • Emergencies are handled on a case-by-case basis — please reach out as soon as possible.
💳

Payment

  • Payment is due before or at the start of each session.
  • Accepted methods: Venmo, Zelle, cash, or card (Stripe link provided on booking confirmation).
  • Bundle packages are paid in full at time of purchase.
  • Invoices are available upon request for business or educational reimbursement.
🎸

Equipment & Materials

  • Students are expected to bring their own instrument to in-person sessions (loaner instruments available with advance notice).
  • Sheet music and supplemental materials are provided digitally at no extra cost.
  • Online students should test their audio/video setup before the first lesson.
🤝

Studio Conduct

  • Respect for all students, instructors, and equipment is expected at all times.
  • Cell phones should be silenced during sessions unless used for recording or reference.
  • Minors (under 18) must have a parent or guardian consent form on file before the first lesson.
  • The studio reserves the right to discontinue lessons if conduct policies are repeatedly violated.
🔒

Privacy & Media

  • Lesson recordings for personal practice are encouraged and welcomed.
  • Any video or audio shared publicly (e.g., social media) requires written consent.
  • Student information is never shared with third parties.
